Before you ask

The questions every owner asks us.

Is the app really free for my gardeners?

Yes. You pay for your plan; every gardener you invite gets the iOS or Android app at no cost, however many you have.

My crew isn't good with technology.

One button in the morning, tick the tasks, take photos. No menus, no training session — most crews are running it the same day.

What if there's no signal at the property?

The app works offline. Tasks, photos and timings sync to your back office the moment the truck gets signal again.

How long until I'm actually running on it?

Add your gardens and crews and your first round is scheduled in under 10 minutes. Recurring visits then generate themselves.

Do I still need accounting software?

For quotes, invoices and getting paid, no — Field Pilot handles it from completed visits. Your accountant still gets clean exports at year end.

Am I locked into a contract?

No contract, no setup fee. Start free, cancel any time, and take your garden data with you.
